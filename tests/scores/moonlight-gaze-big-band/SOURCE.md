## Notable features

18 parts: piano (grand staff), guitar, bass, drum kit (percussion clef,
unpitched notes, x noteheads), 2 alto, 2 tenor, and baritone saxes,
4 trumpets, 4 trombones, and bass trombone.
Transposing B♭/E♭ parts, key changes, first and second endings, tuplets,
dynamics, hairpins, and fermatas.
The rhythm section is written out in full:
there are **no chord symbols, slash notation, or open solos**.
