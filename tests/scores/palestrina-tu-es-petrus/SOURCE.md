## Files

- `Pal-petdwn5.mxl`: compressed MusicXML 3.0, direct export from Sibelius 7.5.1 (2017-10-23)
- `Pal-petdwn5.pdf`: score, 6 pages (printed 2011-02-18)
- `Pal-petdwn5.sib`: Sibelius 6.1 source
- `Pal-petdwn5.mid`: MIDI, format 1, 7 tracks

All four are the one CPDL edition, kept byte-for-byte under their distributed names.
The PDF and the MusicXML were exported from the `.sib` at different times,
so page layout differs (the MusicXML's credits number 7 pages);
the 84 measures and the opening bars agree.

## Notable for testing

Six separate parts with Latin partbook names
(CANTUS, QUINTUS, ALTUS, TENOR, SEXTUS, BASSUS) and lyrics in every voice,
staggered imitative entries, long melismas with `<extend>`, ties across barlines,
treble-8 clefs, 2/2, one flat, final fermatas, "Prima pars" text.
The PDF has incipit staves with the original clefs; the MusicXML does not encode them.
Lyric `number` attributes are Sibelius-style (`part1verse1` … `part6verse1`), not integers.
