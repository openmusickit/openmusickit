# Swan Lake Suite, Op.20a: No.1, Scène (Moderato)

Composer: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky.
Editor/engraver: Eugene Gajewski, 2024 (typeset in Dorico; IMSLP lists it under "Version A" of the suite).
Category: orchestral, one short movement (70 bars, about 3 minutes).

## Files

- `Swan Lake Suite Op20a - Full score - 01 Flow 1.mxl`: compressed MusicXML 4.0 (Dorico 5.1 export) of No.1 only,
  taken byte-for-byte from the zip
- `PMLP59697-Swan_Lake_Suite_Op20a-pp3-14.pdf`: the Scène only, 12 pages,
  extracted with `qpdf --pages ... 3-14` from the 104-page full-suite PDF
  (sha256 of the original: e500afad8ebc3dc1f5c59d3f557e0442d087739e325c31758cafab902da6ca0f)

The zip's other six movements and the `.dorico` project were not kept,
nor were the suite PDF's title page, contents page and later movements.

## Notable for testing

25 parts: clarinets in A, four horns in F, trumpets in F, harp on a grand staff, full strings.
Many string tremolos, harp tuplets, second voices, hairpins, fermatas, rehearsal marks.
The PDF puts pairs of players on one staff (e.g. Flute I–II),
but the MusicXML has one part per player.

## Caveats

The MusicXML inside the zip is dated 2024-06-05 16:51;
the PDF was created about three hours later (19:41 PDT the same day),
so small editorial differences between the two are possible.
