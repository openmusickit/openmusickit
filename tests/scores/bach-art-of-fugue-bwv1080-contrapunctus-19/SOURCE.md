## Notable for testing

Four-voice fugue in open score, one voice per staff (treble, treble, alto clef, bass);
the parts are named only P1–P4.
D minor, cut time, 288 bars.
Riemann's hairpins and dynamics, grace notes, many ties,
and text labels marking subject entries ("Dux 1", "Comes 2", "Tema 1 invers. in G").

## Caveats

Bach's autograph breaks off partway through;
the closing section is Riemann's completion, not Bach.
The MusicXML is dated 2026-03-26 and the PDF 2026-03-29,
so small differences between the two are possible.
